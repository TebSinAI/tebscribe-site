Questions people actually ask

Frequently asked

Are there free medical calculators?

Yes. Free clinical score calculators are on tebiq.com, with the published interpretation for each score. They are for clinicians and support, but do not replace, clinical judgment.

Are AI tools for doctors safe to use with patient data?

Do not enter patient-identifying information into ChatGPT or any general AI chat. The GPTs listed here run in ChatGPT (OpenAI), and they are not medical devices and do not diagnose.

What is tebCalc?

tebCalc brings clinical score calculators into ChatGPT and Claude, so the score comes from one calculator engine rather than being guessed by the model.

Is there a free dictation tool?

Yes. tebDictate turns your phone into a microphone for any computer, free and end-to-end encrypted.
