The infrastructure for clinical intelligence.

tebIQ is the first surface. Underneath it sits a platform built for trust, privacy, and reasoning at clinical-grade — designed to extend across the entire clinical workflow.

  • Privacy as architecture. On-device transcription on the free tier. Patient audio that never has to leave the phone.
  • Reasoning, not transcription. Clinical scaffolding built in: screening tools, scoring, and attestation that propose and confirm.
  • Specialty-aware by design. Emergency medicine is the founding use case, but the engine generalizes.
  • Built by the user. The roadmap is a working physician's own pain backlog.
  • Engineered, not assembled. Purpose-built models, on-device STT, encryption at rest, audit logging, and role-based access.
  • Get home an hour sooner. Every design decision serves one outcome: the documentation stops winning.

Trust in clinical AI has to be earned.

We think you earn it by being the person who has to live with the thing you built. That belief shapes every product decision at tebScribe.

  • Private by default. Free-tier dictation is transcribed on-device.
  • Built by a clinician, not for one. It was built by the doctor — still on the schedule, still using it on shift.
  • You are always the clinician. tebScribe builds documentation tools, not diagnosis. The clinician signs the note.
  • Earned, shipped, repeated. We ship the unglamorous infrastructure — encryption, audit logging, access controls.
