How MelloBell was born

Our family app is called Bellody, and the B stands for Bella. Bella was very proud of this. She mentioned it at breakfast. She mentioned it at dinner. She may have mentioned it to the mail carrier.

Her little sister Melody was not impressed. "Where's my app?" she asked, arms folded, with the steady stare of someone who has been wronged by a whole software company.

It was a fair question. Bella had an initial on an app. Melody had nothing but a strong sense of justice.

So we went back to the drawing board, which in our house is the kitchen table. Melody wanted a place to play. Bella wanted to make sure she was still in it. After some careful negotiation (and one snack break), the two names met in the middle: Mel from Melody, Bell from Bella.

MelloBell was born: an arcade full of the kind of games we grew up with, made new and safe for our girls and yours. Melody approves. Bella says she approves too, as long as everyone remembers who came first.
