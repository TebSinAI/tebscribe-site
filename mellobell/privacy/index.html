Privacy Policy

MelloBell does not collect any information about you or your child. No accounts, no ads, no tracking, and the games make no internet connection.

Effective [date set on approval] · Applies to MelloBell Kids Arcade ("MelloBell") for iPhone and iPad, published by TebScribe LLC ("we", "us").

The short version

  • No account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics, no crash reporting. Our App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected".
  • Everything stays on the device: scores, saved games and settings.
  • The camera is used only to play face games. Nothing is recorded or leaves the device.
  • Grown-ups only: buying, sharing, links and settings sit behind a parent check.

1. What we collect

Nothing. We run no server that receives anything from MelloBell. The app contains no advertising, analytics, attribution or crash-reporting software from us or anyone else. We never receive names, emails, birthdays, photos, recordings, locations, device identifiers or play data.

2. What stays on the device

Game progress and settings, such as scores, saved games, the chosen controller style and sound settings, are stored only on the device. Deleting the app deletes them.

3. Camera (face games only)

The camera is used only while a face game is open, to follow face movements such as opening the mouth or nodding. Camera images and face shapes are processed in the device's memory and discarded straight away. MelloBell cannot take photos or videos, and nothing is saved or sent anywhere. You can turn the camera off in iPhone Settings; every other game still works.

4. Nearby play (off unless a parent turns it on)

With a parent's permission, MelloBell can find another nearby device running MelloBell over local Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, so two children can play together. Only the game moves, plus a nickname and picture chosen by the parent, pass directly between the two devices. There is no chat and no free text, and nothing goes through the internet or through us. A parent can turn it off at any time.

5. Purchases

MelloBell Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple. We never see payment details. If your family has Bellody Plus, MelloBell checks a setting our Bellody app keeps on the same device to unlock Pro; that check never leaves the device.

6. Children's privacy

MelloBell is made for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because we collect nothing, there is nothing to share, sell or delete. If you believe information was somehow collected, contact us and we will help.

7. Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date above.
