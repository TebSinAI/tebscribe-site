Questions people actually ask

Frequently asked

What apps does Dr. C make?

TebScribe LLC makes tebIQ, an AI medical scribe; tebDictate, a free medical dictation app; and tebChart, a discharge instructions app for iPhone. tebIQ Academy, clinical note practice for international medical graduates, is coming soon.

Which app is free?

tebDictate is free with no account, and tebChart is free with no account. tebIQ is free to start.

Who is each app for?

tebIQ is for clinicians, tebDictate is for anyone who would rather talk than type, and tebChart is for patients and caregivers after a hospital visit.

How is my data handled?

Encrypted in transit and at rest (AES). Never used for training. Never sold.
