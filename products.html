TebScribe LLC · Product family

One company. Three products. Every one of them built by a practicing ER doctor.

TebScribe LLC is a New York limited liability company founded by Dr. Mohammad Chelehmalzadeh, a practicing emergency medicine physician. It builds three products: tebIQ, an AI clinical scribe that turns the patient encounter into a structured note; tebChart, a private health-records app that keeps a patient's own discharge papers and medications organized on their iPhone; and tebDictate, a free tool that turns any phone into a wireless microphone for any computer.

Questions people actually ask

Frequently asked

Are tebIQ, tebChart, and tebDictate the same company?

Yes. All three are products of TebScribe LLC, a New York limited liability company founded by Dr. Mohammad Chelehmalzadeh, a practicing emergency medicine physician. tebIQ is the clinician product, tebChart is the patient product, and tebDictate is a free dictation tool anyone can use.

What is tebIQ?

tebIQ is an AI medical scribe for clinicians. It captures the patient encounter and generates a structured clinical note — history, medical decision making, assessment and plan — shaped for the specialty the clinician actually practices. It runs in a web browser, on iPhone, and on Apple Watch. Encrypted in transit and at rest (AES). Never used for training. Never sold.

What is tebDictate, and is it really free?

tebDictate turns any phone into a wireless microphone for any computer. You open the page on your computer, scan the QR code with your phone, and speak — the text appears on the computer. It is free to use, requires no account and no installation, and the connection is end-to-end encrypted. Speech is recognized on your phone.

Who is TebScribe built for?

tebIQ is for clinicians — physicians, residents, advanced practice providers, and the clinics they work in. tebChart is for patients and families organizing their own records after a hospital or emergency department visit. tebDictate is for anyone, clinical or not, who would rather talk than type.

Where can I get the apps?

tebIQ is available in a web browser at tebiq.com and on the App Store for iPhone and Apple Watch. tebDictate works in any phone browser at tebdictate.com — nothing to install. The tebChart iPhone app has not been released yet; tebchart.com is the place to follow it.
