A private family organizer for iPhone: schedule, school, chores, co-parenting and caregivers

Bellody is a private family organizer for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch from TebScribe. It keeps the invisible list every parent carries — who has practice today, whose form is due, who is picking up whom — with no account, no ads and no tracking.

It includes a shared custody schedule and swap requests for co-parenting, time-limited caregiver access you can revoke in one tap, and the whole household: school, meals, chores, the grocery list and every code for the house. Understanding runs on your iPhone.

Co-parenting is free on both phones, so the other parent never pays. Bellody Plus is one price for the whole family, shared with up to six people through Apple Family Sharing: $19.99 a year, $2.99 a month or $79.99 once (US prices).

Why I made Bellody

Bellody is my way of not losing track of the important dates and the fun times with my family and kids. My wife and I both work full time in healthcare, and we absolutely can't drop the ball with our kids, so I designed it for co-parenting. I also wanted our kids to have direct, headache-free connections with us, through games, wish lists, puzzles and private messages: something personal, without Google, Facebook or Microsoft capturing every moment for their own benefit.

Everything in Bellody is yours, by design. I made this, and I'm not interested in your data. Your private data is yours and yours alone. I'm a physician, and I deeply believe in privacy.
