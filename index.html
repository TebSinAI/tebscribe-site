The infrastructure for clinical intelligence.

tebIQ is the first surface. Underneath it sits a platform built for trust, privacy, and reasoning at clinical-grade — designed to extend across the entire clinical workflow.

  • Privacy as architecture. On-device transcription on the free tier. Patient audio that never has to leave the phone.
  • Reasoning, not transcription. Clinical scaffolding built in: screening tools, scoring, and attestation that propose and confirm.
  • Specialty-aware by design. Emergency medicine is the founding use case, but the engine generalizes.
  • Built by the user. The roadmap is a working physician's own pain backlog.
  • Engineered, not assembled. Purpose-built models, on-device STT, encryption at rest, audit logging, and role-based access.
  • Get home an hour sooner. Every design decision serves one outcome: the documentation stops winning.

Overview

AI medical scribe, free medical dictation and tools for doctors, built by an ER physician

TebScribe LLC is a New York software company founded by a practicing emergency medicine physician. It makes an ambient AI medical scribe for clinical documentation, a free medical dictation app, a discharge instructions app for patients, a free roadmap for international medical graduates, and free medical calculators. Every clinical tool is built around one rule: the clinician reviews and signs the note.

tebIQ: ambient AI medical scribe for ER doctors, hospitalists and clinics

tebIQ listens to the patient encounter and drafts the note: a SOAP note, or history, medical decision making, assessment and plan for emergency medicine, shaped for the specialty. It is an AI scribe for emergency medicine first, and it fits urgent care and hospital medicine too. It also supports nurse notes. tebIQ runs in a web browser, on iPhone, on Apple Watch and on Android, and the clinician edits and signs every note.

tebiq.com App Store Google Play About tebIQ

tebRounds: hospitalist rounding board and discharge summaries

tebRounds is the hospitalist view of tebIQ. Your team's list opens as a rounding board, what is gating each discharge is written down where everyone can see it, and the discharge summary is drafted from the stay you already documented. A physician reads and signs it before it goes anywhere. It is in pilot with hospitalist groups.

About tebRounds

tebDictate: free medical dictation app, voice to text for doctors and nurses

tebDictate turns your phone into a wireless microphone for any computer. Open tebdictate.com on the computer, scan the QR code with your phone, and speak: the words appear in whatever text box is open. It is free, needs no account and nothing to install, speech is recognized on your phone, and the connection is end-to-end encrypted. If a hospital computer blocks it, ask your IT team.

tebdictate.com App Store How free dictation works

tebChart: discharge instructions app and after visit summary organizer

tebChart is a discharge instructions app for iPhone, made for patients and caregivers after a hospital or emergency department visit. Scan your discharge papers and it organizes the after visit summary, your medication list, follow-up visits and the questions worth asking. Your records stay on your own iPhone, with no health records on TebScribe servers.

tebchart.com App Store About tebChart

IMG roadmap: from foreign medical graduate to U.S. residency

A free 10-part guide for international medical graduates, written by an emergency physician. It follows the pathway from medical school abroad to a U.S. license: ECFMG certification, the USMLE, OET, U.S. clinical experience, ERAS and the Match, what to do if you do not match, newer state pathways for experienced physicians, visas for doctors, and a master checklist. Requirements change, so confirm them with ECFMG, NRMP and USCIS. This is general information, not legal or immigration advice.

Frequently asked questions

What is an AI medical scribe?

An AI medical scribe listens to the patient encounter and drafts the clinical documentation for the clinician to review and sign. tebIQ is an ambient AI scribe for doctors: it turns the visit into a structured note, a SOAP note or history, medical decision making, assessment and plan for emergency medicine, shaped for the specialty. It runs in a web browser, on iPhone, on Apple Watch and on Android, and it also supports nurse notes. The clinician is always responsible for the note.

Is there an AI scribe for ER doctors and emergency medicine?

Yes. tebIQ was built by a practicing emergency physician and emergency medicine is its founding use case. It drafts the ER note from the encounter, including history, medical decision making, assessment and plan, and the physician edits and signs it. It also fits urgent care and hospital medicine.

Is there a free medical dictation app?

Yes. tebDictate is a free medical dictation app. Open tebdictate.com on your computer, scan the QR code with your phone, and use voice to text to dictate to any computer. Your phone is the wireless microphone and the speech to text happens on the phone. It needs no account and nothing to install, works for nurse dictation and doctor dictation alike, and the connection is end-to-end encrypted. How free dictation works.

How do I organize discharge instructions and my after visit summary?

Scan your discharge papers with tebChart, a discharge instructions app for iPhone. It organizes the after visit summary, a medication list, follow-up visits and the questions worth asking, so a hospital discharge is easier to follow for you and for a caregiver. Records stay on your own device, with no health records on TebScribe servers.

What is tebRounds?

tebRounds is the hospitalist view of tebIQ. The team's list opens as a rounding board, what is gating each discharge is written down where the team can see it, and the discharge summary is drafted from the stay already documented for a physician to review and sign. It is the same tebIQ app and account, in pilot with hospitalist groups. About tebRounds.

Is there a free roadmap for foreign medical graduates who want to practice in the U.S.?

Yes. The IMG roadmap is a free 10-part guide for international medical graduates, written by an emergency physician. It covers ECFMG certification, the USMLE, OET, U.S. clinical experience, ERAS and the Match, what to do if you do not match, practicing without repeating residency, visas for doctors and a master checklist. Requirements change, so confirm current rules with ECFMG, NRMP and USCIS. Open the IMG roadmap.

Where can I find free medical calculators?

The free medical tools page lists clinical score calculators such as HEART, Wells, CURB-65 and NEWS2, free dictation, and free educational GPTs for the emergency department, clinic, patient education and study. They support, and do not replace, clinical judgment. Browse the free medical tools.

No. TebScribe builds documentation tools, not diagnosis. The clinician reviews and signs every note and is always responsible for it.
