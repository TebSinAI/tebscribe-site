More than a product · A platform

The infrastructure for clinical intelligence.

tebIQ is the first surface. Underneath it sits a platform built for trust, privacy, and reasoning at clinical-grade — designed to extend across the entire clinical workflow.

Privacy as architecture

On-device transcription on the free tier. Patient audio that never has to leave the phone. Privacy isn't a setting — it's the foundation everything is built on.

Reasoning, not transcription

Clinical scaffolding built in: screening tools, scoring, and attestation that propose and confirm. The hard part — the defensible decision-making — happens in the product.

Specialty-aware by design

Emergency medicine is the founding use case, but the engine generalizes. Templates and clinical logic adapt to the specialty, not the other way around.

Built by the user

The roadmap is a working physician's own pain backlog. When something breaks on a real shift, it gets fixed for everyone. No advisory board — the doctor is on the schedule.

Engineered, not assembled

Purpose-built models, on-device STT, encryption at rest, audit logging, and role-based access — the kind of infrastructure healthcare actually requires, built in from day one.

Get home an hour sooner

Every design decision serves one outcome: the documentation stops winning. Clinicians finish their notes on shift — and get their evenings back.

Why we're different

Trust in clinical AI has to be earned.

We think you earn it by being the person who has to live with the thing you built. That belief shapes every product decision at tebScribe.

Private by default

Free-tier dictation is transcribed on-device. We designed for the clinician holding the phone in the room with the patient — and we weren't comfortable with anything less.

Built by a clinician, not for one

tebIQ wasn't specced by a growth team with a doctor on the advisory board. It was built by the doctor — still on the schedule, still using it on shift this weekend.

You are always the clinician

tebScribe builds documentation tools, not diagnosis. The product supports your judgment and reasoning — it never replaces it. The clinician signs the note.

Earned, shipped, repeated

We ship the unglamorous infrastructure — encryption, audit logging, access controls — because that's what earns the right to handle clinical work at scale.
