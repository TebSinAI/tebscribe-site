tebRounds

The hospitalist view of tebIQ · In pilot

tebRounds is the hospitalist view of tebIQ. Your team's list opens as a rounding board instead of a search box. What's gating each discharge gets written down where the team can see it. The discharge summary is drafted from the stay you already documented — and a physician reads and signs it before it goes anywhere.

tebRounds isn't a separate app or a separate download — it's the same tebIQ app and account, with inpatient features rolling out to pilot groups. There's no store listing for it on its own.
