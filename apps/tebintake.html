tebIntake

Multilingual front-desk intake · Coming soon

An offline, multilingual patient-intake kiosk for front desks and clinics. It captures a patient's history in their own language and drafts a plain-language summary for staff to review before it's used — the patient's own words stay attached to the summary, and a staff member always reads it back before anything is final.
