Free medical dictation: your phone is the microphone

tebDictate is a free medical dictation app: open it on a computer, scan the QR code with your phone, and speak. The words appear on the computer, in any text box. Voice to text for doctors, nurses and clinicians, end-to-end encrypted.

How it works

Three steps. No account, nothing to install.

  1. Open tebdictate.comOn the computer where you want the text to appear.
  2. Scan the QR codeUse the camera on your phone. Your phone becomes the wireless microphone.
  3. SpeakYour words type themselves into whatever text box is in front of you.

Who uses it

For doctors, nurses, clinicians — and anyone who would rather talk than type.

tebDictate is a free medical dictation app: a voice to text tool for doctors, a dictation app for nurses, and a dictation tool for anyone else. It was built by Dr. C (Dr. Mohammad Chelehmalzadeh, emergency physician), who wanted to dictate into the charting window he already had open.

Questions people actually ask

Frequently asked

Is the free medical dictation app really free?

Yes. tebDictate is free to use, with no account and nothing to install.

Does it work on hospital computers?

It runs in the browser, so there is nothing to install. If a site blocks it, ask your IT team. Details are at tebdictate.com/privacy.

Which languages does it support?

Speech is recognized on your phone, so the languages available follow what your phone's own dictation supports. Check tebdictate.com for the current list.

Is my dictation private?

The connection between your phone and your computer is end-to-end encrypted, and nothing readable is stored.
